The Workshop Collective

Welcome to the Southern Highlands' upcoming community hub built by makers, for makers. We are establishing a dedicated workshop space paired with a vibrant social atmosphere and onsite ramen food trailer.

Our vision is simple: provide a fully equipped, collaborative environment where car enthusiasts, woodworkers, fabricators, and creators of all skill levels can access proper tools, share expertise, and work on projects side by side.

Mental Health & Wellbeing: We believe that working with your hands and connecting with others is powerful medicine. By providing a safe, supportive space and sharing practical knowledge from experienced mentors, we foster genuine community connection, reduce isolation, and support mental wellbeing through hands-on collaboration.
